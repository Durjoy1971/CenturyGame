# CenturyGame
In this 1 vs 1 game, the Centurion will be the player who reaches a score of 100 first. Each player rolls a dice to score runs, except when rolling a 1, which means the player is out and earns no points. Players have the option to hold their current score, adding it to their total, and passing the turn to their opponent. The first player to reach 100 points wins the game.
